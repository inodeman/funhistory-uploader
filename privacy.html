Privacy Policy – FunHistory Uploader

Last updated: October 26, 2026

FunHistory Uploader (the "App") is a Google OAuth application operated by Jaime Enriquez, owner of the FunHistoryCartoons YouTube channel.

What the App does

The App is used solely by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, and to read the channel's YouTube Analytics data to understand video performance.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization via Google OAuth, the App accesses: the owner's YouTube channel information, videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy settings), and YouTube Analytics reports for that channel. OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to perform these actions.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the App's upload, management, and analytics features for the owner's own channel. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

No user data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

FunHistory Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the App's features. You can revoke the App's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and may request deletion of any stored data by email.

Changes

This policy may be updated; changes will be posted on this page.
